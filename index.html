    What We Do

    ⚡ Lightning Talks

    Short, high-impact presentations from local practitioners on emerging tools, techniques, and lessons learned.

    🎤 Technical Talks

    In-depth sessions from industry experts covering AI, software architecture, cloud infrastructure, and more.

    🔧 Workshops

    Hands-on sessions that build real skills — from prompt engineering to system design to DevOps practices.

    🌐 Networking

    Connect with Richmond's tech community, find collaborators, mentors, and career opportunities.

    Why Join Us

    💼

    For Professionals

    Share your expertise with the Richmond tech community, expand your professional network, and connect with peers driving innovation across industries. Every event is a platform to grow your presence and make a meaningful contribution.

    🏫

    For Learners

    Gain exposure to real-world technology through expert talks and hands-on workshops. Connect with mentors, discover career paths, and develop skills that go beyond any classroom or certification.

    🤝

    For the Community

    Help bridge the gap between industry and academia in Greater Richmond. By participating, you foster a culture of collaboration, inclusion, and innovation that benefits the entire regional tech ecosystem.
