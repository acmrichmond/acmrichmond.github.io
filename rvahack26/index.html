    What Is RVAHack26?

    RVAHack26 is a free, three-day international hackathon hosted by ACM Richmond in partnership with IEEE Computer Society and IEEE Richmond Section. Because it's fully virtual, participants anywhere in the world can form teams, build a project from scratch, and present it to a panel of judges — no travel required.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is RVAHack26 really open internationally?

    Yes — the entire event is virtual, so participants, mentors, and judges from anywhere in the world are welcome to participate.

    Is there a cost to participate?

    No, RVAHack26 is completely free for participants, mentors, and judges.

    Do I need a team before I register?

    No. Teams are formed during the Day 1 inauguration if you don't already have one.
