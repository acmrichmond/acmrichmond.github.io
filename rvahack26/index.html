    What Is RVAHack26?

    RVAHack26 was a free, three-day international hackathon on the theme AI for Software Engineering, hosted by ACM Richmond, IEEE Richmond Section, IEEE Computer Society, and IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society (TEMS). Fully virtual and open worldwide, it kicked off with an in-person inauguration in Richmond, Virginia on Friday, October 30, then moved online for two days of building and judging.

    Track & Judging

    This year's theme

    AI for Software Engineering

    Tools and applications that use AI to improve how software is designed, built, tested, or maintained.

    7 categories 1–5 per category 35 points max 14 judges per project
    1. ⚙️

      Technical Complexity

      How challenging, complete, and well-engineered is the build?

    2. 🤖

      Use of AI

      How meaningfully does AI make this project possible or better for software engineering?

    3. 💡

      Originality & Innovation

      How novel, creative, or outside the box is the idea?

    4. 🎯

      Impact & Usefulness

      How likely is it that real developers or teams would adopt this and benefit?

    5. ♿

      Accessibility (UI/UX)

      How well can the widest possible audience use and understand it?

    6. 🎤

      Presentation

      How well did the team explain what it is, why it matters, how it was built, and show it working?

    7. 🤝

      Inclusivity & Collaboration

      How well do the project and the team reflect inclusive thinking and teamwork?

    1 = needs significant work  ·  3 = solid  ·  5 = excellent  (2 and 4 for work in between). Final rankings used each project's average score across its 14 judges.
